Press kit

Text, logos and images you can use when you write about STBLpay, for media, bloggers and partners. There is also an official button and mark for merchant sites. When you introduce the service in an article, please use the materials on this page as they are.

Updated: November 6, 2026 · By Ango-ya LLC

Boilerplate

Short (about 40 words)

STBLpay is a payment service under development: buyers pay in yen, and merchants receive the stablecoin JPYC. Buyers approve each payment with a passkey (face or fingerprint), and merchants do not rely on card companies' credit screening.

Long (about 120 words)

STBLpay (すてぶるペイ, pronounced "stable pay") is a payment service under development by Ango-ya LLC of Fukuoka, Japan. Buyers pay in yen, and merchants receive JPYC, a stablecoin pegged to the Japanese yen. Buyers approve each payment with a passkey (face or fingerprint). When their JPYC balance is enough, no bank transfer is needed; when it is not, they issue JPYC with a Japanese-yen bank transfer and pay with it. Merchants receive sales in JPYC without relying on card companies' credit screening. The design is non-custodial: the provider holds no funds or private keys of buyers or merchants. Fees are under consideration, with a single flat rate for all merchants planned and nothing added for buyers. A Sandbox, merchant docs and an OpenAPI 3.1 specification are public.

For merchants: official button and mark

For shops that accept STBLpay, we provide an official "Pay with STBLpay" button and a mark for your list of payment methods. They help buyers tell STBLpay apart from other payment methods, so please use them. Host the files on your own server (they cannot be loaded directly from this site).

Official button

Put the CSS and the white logo (the SVG under Logo downloads) on your own server, then write the following. Adjust the paths to where you put the files.

<link rel="stylesheet" href="/stblpay/stblpay-button.css">

<button type="button" class="stblpay-btn stblpay-btn--en">

Writing the name

  • In English text, write "STBLpay": STBL in capitals, pay in lowercase, with no space. Do not write "STBL Pay", "StblPay", "STBLPAY" or "Stable Pay".
  • It is pronounced "stable pay".
  • In Japanese text, the name is すてぶるペイ (hiragana すてぶる plus katakana ペイ), read ステーブルペイ. Do not split it as "すてぶる ペイ".
  • To show both names, write "STBLpay (すてぶるペイ)".
  • The operator is "Ango-ya LLC" (合同会社暗号屋 in Japanese).
  • The service is under development. Please say so when you introduce it.
