Guides to payments in Japan and stablecoins

These guides are for overseas online shops that sell to customers in Japan, and for anyone researching yen stablecoins. They explain how people in Japan pay online, how konbini payment works, what a yen stablecoin such as JPYC is, and how a business can accept stablecoin payments. Each page answers its question in the first paragraph, and facts about laws and other companies come with sources and the date we checked them.

Updated: November 9, 2026　By Ango-ya LLC

  • Payment Methods in Japan: How Customers in Japan Pay Online

    Payment methods in Japan: cards, konbini, bank transfer, cash on delivery, carrier billing, QR code and pay-later, and what an overseas shop should know.

  • What Is Konbini Payment? Convenience-Store Payments in Japan Explained

    Konbini payment lets buyers in Japan pay cash at a convenience store for online orders. See how it works, limits, expiry, refunds and alternatives.

  • Accepting Payments from Customers in Japan with a Yen Stablecoin

    How can an overseas online shop accept payments from customers in Japan? A yen stablecoin such as JPYC is one option. This guide covers Japan's payment landscape, how the law treats yen stablecoins, and how STBLpay (under development) works.

  • How to Accept Stablecoin Payments as a Business

    How to accept stablecoin payments as a business: wallet or payment gateway, steps to start, costs, risks, and where a yen stablecoin like JPYC fits.

  • What Is a Yen Stablecoin? JPYC and Japan's Stablecoin Rules Explained

    A yen stablecoin is a digital token tied to the Japanese yen. Learn what JPYC is, how Japan regulates stablecoins, and how they differ from USDC and USDT.

  • What Is a High-Risk Merchant Account? A Plain-English Guide

    A high-risk merchant account is a card account for businesses that processors see as higher risk. Learn why, what it involves, and the alternatives.

About STBLpay

STBLpay (すてぶるペイ) is a payment service in which buyers pay in yen and merchants receive JPYC (under development). Buyers pay by Japanese-yen bank transfer and approve with a passkey, and merchants receive sales without relying on card companies' credit screening.
